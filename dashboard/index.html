How the value is worked out

Each protocol’s implied value comes from its own figures in the snapshot, and from what the market already pays for the same kind of protocol:

  1. Peer multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-over-TVL of the live-token protocols in that category, shrunk toward the global median with a prior of 10, then clamped between 0.05 and 1.50. The strong prior matters: some categories have a single live peer (Risk Curators: only Api3 at 2.97×; Prediction Market: only Rain at 400.2×), so a raw category median would be one protocol's price, not a market.
  2. Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100%, moves the value by half its size.
  3. Maturity. A protocol tracked longer counts for more: 0.70 for a newborn, up to 1.00 after four years.
  4. Reach. Each extra chain beyond the first adds 2%, up to four.

implied = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × reach
